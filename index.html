The 3D globe needs WebGL 2. Everything this page is built from is listed under "Data sources".

The surface is NASA's Blue Marble imagery (shaded relief and bathymetry) from NASA GIBS, drawn on a WGS84 ellipsoid and lit by the Sun's real position for the simulated time. The atmosphere is computed, not painted. The starfield is decorative, not a star map.
